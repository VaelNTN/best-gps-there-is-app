# Privacy Policy — Best GPS There Is

**Last updated:** 2026-11-17

Best GPS There Is respects your privacy:

- **Location Data:** Your GPS coordinates are used locally on your device to show your position on the map, provide turn-by-turn voice guidance, and request routes from the selected map/routing provider (OpenStreetMap/Valhalla/Photon, or OpenRouteService/HERE if you configure your own key).
- **Local Storage:** Vehicle dimensions, favorite places, recent searches, language, and settings are stored strictly locally on your device (`localStorage`).
- **No Tracking or Ad Networks:** The app contains zero advertising SDKs, zero analytics trackers, and zero account requirements.
